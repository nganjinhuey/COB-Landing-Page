      What this means for you

      • Most visits are for common illnesses such as coughs, colds, sore throats and sinus infections. These are all covered under COB.
      • Each clinic visit is supported up to RM120 per episode, and you get up to 8 episodes a year.
      • You can also see a doctor online through teleconsultation, 9am to 10pm daily, without going to a clinic. How it works

      About these figures

      • Figures come from the monthly COB reports prepared with our healthcare partners HEYDOC Health and MiCare.
      • Active members are members whose COB was active during that month.
      • A member who visited a clinic more than once in a month is counted once under "Members who used COB".
      • The report is updated monthly.
